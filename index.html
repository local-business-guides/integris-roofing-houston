Define the Project Before Work Begins

A roofing scope should make the intended work understandable. Request a description of the affected sections, the materials involved, and the tasks included in the proposal. If something is unclear, ask for clarification before accepting the scope.

Useful questions for a roofing discussion include:

  • Which findings support the recommended work?
  • Does the proposal cover a repair or a broader roofing project?
  • What preparation is needed at the property?
  • How will any additional findings be discussed?
  • Who should receive questions about the project?

These questions apply to both homes and commercial buildings, although access arrangements may differ. A business property may need coordination with a building manager or occupants. A residential project may involve gates, parking, pets, or belongings close to the building.
